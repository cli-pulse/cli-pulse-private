Codex cost estimates are higher in this version, and Claude estimates change. CLI Pulse on your Mac counts and prices your usage, so most of these figures change on iPhone once your Mac runs this version and syncs.

• Codex cost estimates are higher. Subagent sessions are now counted; they used to be left out. And each request is priced at the API price OpenAI lists for its model on the day it was made: a price change applies from its date, and newer models such as gpt-5.5 and gpt-5.6-sol have their own prices instead of gpt-5.4's.

• Older Codex days are counted again under the new rules. If you allowed CLI Pulse on your Mac to read older logs, it recounts the older Codex days it has already synced, so older days in the Usage Dashboard show the new figures too. Some days keep their earlier figures, for example when their logs have been deleted.

• The Usage Dashboard now counts Codex's cached input once. Cached input is already part of Codex's input but was added a second time, which made the Codex token totals there too high, on every day. Costs were not affected.

• Claude cost estimates for the last 30 days change as well: CLI Pulse on your Mac now prices Claude at Anthropic's list prices for the day, with their own prices for Claude Opus 5.5, Claude Fable 5.1 and Claude Sonnet 5.5, and counts the full output of each response, so estimates go down for some models and up for others.

• Try Demo now looks like a single real refresh: its alerts, quotas, devices and Cost Summary are ones a real account can show, and the Cost Summary rows add up to its total.

• A provider's overall Quota bar now fills to the share left, like the other bars.
