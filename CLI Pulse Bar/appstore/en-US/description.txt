CLI Pulse keeps track of your AI coding tools: how much of each quota you have used, when it resets and what your usage is costing you. It works with Claude, Codex, Gemini, Cursor, Copilot, OpenRouter, Ollama and dozens of other providers, on your Mac and on your iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch.

ON YOUR MAC
- Your providers' quotas and reset times, one click away in the menu bar
- Pace: whether your quota will last until it resets, or when it will run out
- Estimated cost from your Claude Code and Codex logs, calculated on your Mac
- Usage history for each provider, an activity heatmap and a cost forecast
- Active and recent sessions by project, with usage and estimated cost
- Alerts when a quota runs low, when usage or cost spikes, or when spending passes a budget you set
- Cost reports you can export as CSV or PDF
- Pulse Cat, an optional desktop cat whose mood follows your real AI usage
- No account needed: in local mode your usage stays on this Mac

ON IPHONE, IPAD AND APPLE WATCH
- The same quotas, costs, sessions and alerts, synced from your Mac
- Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets (Pro)
- Siri and Shortcuts: ask for today's status or for one provider's remaining quota
- Apple Watch: quota rings, alerts and sessions on your wrist, plus complications

The iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch apps show what CLI Pulse collects on your Mac. Install it on your Mac and sign in with the same account on each device. Try Demo shows the app with sample data before you connect a Mac.

PRIVACY
- Your provider API keys and the contents of your session logs never leave your Mac
- When you sign in, usage figures sync to your CLI Pulse account over an encrypted connection
- No ads and no third-party analytics SDKs

FREE AND PRO
CLI Pulse is free for up to three providers. CLI Pulse Pro adds unlimited providers and the Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets.

SUBSCRIPTION INFORMATION

You can manage and cancel your subscriptions by going to your account settings on the App Store after purchase.
