# v1.63.0 release notes

**Marketing version**: 1.63.0
**Apple CFBundleVersion**: 118
**Android versionCode**: 84. Unlike 1.62, Android ships with this release, on
Google Play and on its own schedule (see *Shipping* below).
**Platforms**: the Mac (Mac App Store, and Developer ID / Homebrew), iPhone
and iPad, Apple Watch, and Android.
**Backend**: nothing to apply. v0.88 (#753) went to production on 2026-11-10,
and v0.89, v0.90 and v0.91 (#760, #761, #763) on 2026-11-12, ahead of this

Theme: **sign-in and privacy correctness**. Signing out ends only the device
you sign out on. On Android, a session ends once, for the right reasons, and
takes its data with it. Crash reports carry less. Each platform's privacy
manifest now lists what its binary actually calls. No new features.

## Apple

### A1: Sign Out ends only this device's session (#759; Mac, iPhone, Watch)

- **Before:** `POST /auth/v1/logout` went out without a `scope`, so it used
  Supabase's default, `global`: one device's Sign Out ended every session of
  the account. An expired access token was sent as it was, refused, and nothing
  was revoked.
- **Now:** the logout carries `scope=local`. A token at or within 60 s of
  expiry is first refreshed with the session's own refresh token. That refresh
  is never installed or written to the Keychain. The revocation is bound to the
  session being left: a sign-in that arrives meanwhile is never touched, and a
  refresh still in flight for the old session is waited for, at most 10 s.
  Best effort, with a 20 s budget.
- **Watch:** a Watch running on the session relayed from the iPhone shares
  that session. Its own Sign Out sends nothing, and the iPhone's Sign Out ends
  it. A Watch signed in with its own code has its own session.

Devices still on 1.62 or earlier, Apple or Android, still sign out globally,
and so sign out a 1.63 device too.

### A2: crash reports drop the device hash and screen titles (#755; Mac, iPhone, Watch)

- `beforeSend` removes `contexts.app.device_app_hash`. sentry-cocoa 9.12.1 sets
  it from the identifier for vendor on iPhone and Watch, and from the en0 MAC
  address on the Mac.
- `beforeBreadcrumb` drops `title` from `ui.lifecycle` and `touch`
  breadcrumbs. Those titles are a Mac's name, a session's title or a paired
  Mac's name.
- `enableMetrics=false`. CLIPulseCore pins Sentry 9.12.1, the same version as
  the apps, and `check_sentry_pins.sh` keeps the two pins equal.

Sentry's random installation ID stays, so the App Store label stays
**Linked**. Before any change to the label or to the policy's device-hash
text, a release build's raw envelopes (a handled event, a crash sent on the
next launch, a session) are checked. See *Before submitting* below.

### A3: privacy manifests match the binaries (#767)

- **Manifests:** new or corrected `PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy` for iOS, Watch,
  Widgets, Mac and the LoginItem. They cover SystemBootTime 35F9.1,
  FileTimestamp C617.1/3B52.1, and UserDefaults CA92.1/1C8F.1.
- **Code:** `LocaleOverrideStore` and `FirstRunPresentation` now read only the
  app's own defaults domain, as CA92.1 requires.
- **Gate:** `scripts/check_privacy_manifests.py` (79 test cases) runs in Repo
  Hygiene. Its known limits are listed in #767.

## Android

- **D1, sessions (#762, #764):**
  - A session ends once, and only when the server rejects it with a reason on
    an allowlist.
  - An expired session is renewed and the request retried. Offline errors,
    5xx and ambiguous answers keep the session.
  - Ending a session clears the encrypted prefs and the six Room tables, and
    the clearing survives the process being killed.
  - Navigation follows the session ID.
  - An OAuth callback claims only its own flow.
- **D2, Sign Out (#765):**
  - Sign Out uses `scope=local`, refreshing first like A1.
  - Delete Account asks the server and then ends that session. It no longer
    sends a separate sign-out.
  - A link exchange revokes the extra server session it creates.
- **D3, privacy policy link (#757):** a Privacy Policy link in Settings and
  on the sign-in screen, which now scrolls.
- **D4, data on the phone (#758):**
  - `data_extraction_rules` keep all app data out of cloud backup and out of
    a device-to-device transfer.
  - Sentry scrubbing also redacts UUIDs and e-mail addresses, and drops URL
    queries and fragments.
  - Release logs carry no device ID.

## Before submitting the Apple build

- **The A2 raw-envelope check:** a release build, pointed at a capture
  endpoint, sends a handled event, a crash on the next launch and a session.
  None of them may carry `device_app_hash` or a `ui.lifecycle`/`touch` title.
  - If the check passes, PRIVACY.md's device-hash and screen-title text
    changes on the Apple release day, and the App Privacy label is reviewed
    with the owner.
  - If the check fails, the What's New crash-report line is removed before
    submission.
- **Manifests:** `find … -name '*.xcprivacy'` on the archive shows every
  product carries its manifest, and Xcode's privacy report matches it (A3's
  plan).
