# v1.54.0 release notes

**Marketing version**: 1.54.0
**Apple CFBundleVersion**: 107
**Android versionCode**: 73 (source only: no Android build is made or
published with this release)
**Backend**: none. No Supabase migrations and no edge functions this train.
**Helper**: no helper release (the only change under `HelperSwift/` is a test, #553).

Theme: **"in the reader's language, and the same number everywhere"**.

---

## Why these notes are short

Nearly all of 1.54.0 is localization: text that still showed English in
Chinese, Japanese, Korean and Spanish, and dates and numbers written the wrong
way for the reader. The rest is a handful of fixes. The App Store "What's New"
texts are in `whatsnew_154/`, separate per platform.

## Both apps

- **Quota percentages agree.** A window 92.1% used said "8% remaining" in its
  alert and "7% left" on its card. Cards, the menu bar, widgets, the Watch and
  Siri now round the way the alert does: used is rounded, left is 100 minus
  used (#596).
- **English that was still showing is translated**: provider status, quota tier
  names, plan badges, alert titles and messages, session names, Demo mode's
  sample alerts. Stored data stays English; it is translated where it is shown
  (#557, #558, #560, #561, #563–#565, #568, #569, #581, #585, #586). Each
  language was then reviewed by a native speaker as a whole (#583, #592, #593,
  #600). Traditional Chinese uses one word each for session, alert and
  provider (#598).
- **Errors** are sentences in the user's language instead of raw server JSON or
  NSError codes (#584).
- **Dates, times, numbers and money** follow the reader's language and region;
  quota reset times are local time, not UTC (#591). Counts of one are singular
  ("1 session") (#590). VoiceOver says what a number means (#587).
- **Non-Gregorian calendars.** With the Japanese, Minguo or Buddhist calendar,
  day keys were built in that calendar: on the Mac, Codex usage and cost read
  zero and daily usage was uploaded under the wrong date; on the iPhone, the
  usage-history chart and the cost forecast read zero. Day keys are now always
  Gregorian (#589).
- **Activity heatmap** is filled in under Try Demo, and no longer shows the
  previous account's activity after switching accounts (#556).
- The bundled images were compressed losslessly, 564 KB smaller (#552).

## iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch and widgets

- Siri and Shortcuts work in all six languages: action names, phrases and
  answers (#567).
- The two existing permission prompts (camera, local network) are translated
  (#566). No Info.plist key and no entitlement changed since 1.53.0.
- Widgets, the Watch and Siri show costs in the currency chosen in the app.
- iOS 26: one ••• button on the Providers tab, the Risk Signals card spans the
  full width, and the hourly chart is titled "Hourly Activity" (#596, #601).

## Mac

- The language menu lists all six languages (한국어 and Español were missing),
  from first launch and while signed out (#588).
- Settings offers Set Up Cloud Sync on a Mac that was removed from, or never
  paired to, an account another device had already paired (#599).
- "Local-only mode" is renamed "Strict privacy mode". Behaviour is unchanged
  (#600).
- The first-launch statistics notice is no longer clipped, Korean no longer
  breaks lines mid-word, and refresh intervals read "5 min", not "5m" (#600).

## Not in the binary

- App Store listing text in all seven store locales (#597), iPhone screenshots
  in six languages (#601, #603) and per-platform What's New (#602). These are
  pushed to App Store Connect during the release.
- Android: the source carries its own localization work (#570–#580, #582, #585,
  #598). Its version fields move with Apple's only because
  `scripts/check-versions.sh` requires it.
- CI gates for hardcoded UI text, strings parity, zh-Hant terms, quota tier
  names, collector status text and alert templates (#557, #562, #570, #573,
  #594, #595, #598), and a fix for a test race that hung CI (#559).
