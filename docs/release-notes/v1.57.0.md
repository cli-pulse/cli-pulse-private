# v1.57.0 release notes

**Marketing version**: 1.57.0
**Apple CFBundleVersion**: 110
**Android versionCode**: 76 (source only: no Android build is made or
published with this release)
**Backend**: no migration and no edge-function deploy. The only deployable
backend file that changed since 1.56.0, `send-webhook/index.ts`, changed in
comments only (#673); `TYPE_ALIASES` still maps `session_long`. The other
change under `backend/` is the CI check `ci_check_alert_types.py` (#673).
Nothing in 1.57 touches the database.
**Companion CLI**: 1.33.0 (`helper/system_collector.py` `HELPER_VERSION`,
HelperSwift `kHelperVersion` and `helper-uninstaller/Info.plist`, all 1.32.0
in 1.56.0), released as the Companion CLI `.pkg` with this release.

Theme: **"one provider's 30 days, the same figures everywhere you see them"**.

---

## What 1.57.0 is

On the Mac, each provider card shows Today, This Week and 30 Days beside a
small 30-day chart, and the chart opens a new provider mode of the slide-out
panel: one provider's last 30 days by day and model, in tokens or cost. The
Codex card can show the account's limit reset credits. Card sections can be
hidden. On iPhone and iPad, the card's chart switches between tokens and cost
and lists a tapped day's models, and the card's Today and This Week count the
chart's own rows. Crof is retired; Kimi K2 and CrossModel are hidden.
Credentialed requests no longer reach a disk cache. Process-scan sessions read
"Running" everywhere and carry no runtime-derived figures.

Costs stay estimates and are labelled that way. There is no Pro gate on any
of it. The App Store "What's New" texts are in `whatsnew_157/`, separate per
platform: the Mac text leaves out what only the Developer ID build has and
what the sandboxed build cannot see (running processes).

## #656: "Session Too Long" no longer fires for every AI tool open five hours

The long-running rule fires at 400 requests, and a process-scan row's request
count is only its runtime divided by 45 s. The rule skipped scan rows by
testing for `proc-`, but the Mac's LoginItem helper names its rows `local-`,
so on a Developer ID Mac every detected AI tool open about five hours (Claude's
desktop app among them) raised the alert. The rule now skips both prefixes in
the app's core, and HelperSwift's copy, which had no skip, skips `proc-`, the
only prefix its scan writes. No producer passes a non-scan session to
either generator, so the rule no longer fires in the shipped app (see #673 for
Integrations). Alert rows already raised stay until they are resolved; nothing
ran against production.

## #664: CI action updates

Dependabot's github-actions group: `actions/setup-java` 5.7.0 → 6.0.1,
`actions/upload-artifact` 7.0.1 → 7.0.2, `gradle/actions/wrapper-validation`
6.3.0 → 6.4.0. No inputs we pass changed meaning. Not in any binary.

## #665: Crof retired, Kimi K2 and CrossModel hidden

- **Crof** is gone from Settings › Providers, the Mac cards, the iPhone and
  the Watch: its service shut down. Its collector is deleted.
- **Kimi K2 and CrossModel** are hidden the same way: both are relays whose
  operator could not be confirmed (Kimi K2's collector read `kimi-k2.ai`, not
  Moonshot's API). Their collectors stay in the source, unregistered, so no
  key is sent to them from the app or the LoginItem helper, which also no
  longer loads their keys. **Kimi** and **Moonshot** stay.
- Saved accounts for the three are left out when configs are read, and the
  first 1.57 launch writes the kept list back. The enum cases and raw values
  stay, so older configs, archived history and rows another device uploads
  still decode. A retired row takes no free-plan slot.
- **A one-time notice** in the Mac popover, in six languages, for someone
  whose key a Kimi K2 or CrossModel collector actually had (a saved key, or an
  environment key its collector read while the account was on). It stays
  until dismissed and never comes back after that.
- **A running process that names Kimi K2 counts as Kimi** in every process
  scan (the app's, the LoginItem helper's, HelperSwift's and the Companion
  CLI's), so no session is labelled with a provider the app no longer shows.

## #666, #669, #670: Codex limit reset credits (Mac)

- The Mac's Codex card shows the account's **limit reset credits**: how many
  are available and when each expires, the soonest first, at most four
  countdowns and then "+N". One line under the quota bars, only when the
  account has a usable credit. Both Mac builds; the iPhone does not show them.
- One read-only `GET` to `chatgpt.com` with the token and account of the same
  `/wham/usage` request that just answered (auth.json is not read again).
  Nothing in CLI Pulse calls the endpoint that redeems one. A 4 s budget,
  fail-isolated: a failure hides the row and leaves the quota alone.
- Kept in memory only, each credit's ID and the ChatGPT account as hashes;
  never written to disk, uploaded, or sent to the iPhone or Watch. Only the
  main app reads them; the LoginItem helper does not.
- The App Store build without the `~/.codex` grant shows "Allow access to
  ~/.codex" on the row instead.
- **#669**: the read no longer sends `originator: Codex Desktop`, a header that
  names OpenAI's own app. On a real account the endpoint answered the same
  credits with and without it, and the count and expiry instants matched
  CodexBar 0.67.0's reading of the same account. An env-gated local check
  (`CodexResetCreditsLocalCheckTests`) can repeat that before a release; it is
  skipped everywhere else.
- **#670**: VoiceOver reads each countdown with the units the chip shows
  ("6 days, 4 hours"), where it used to round the last unit.

## #667: the usage panel's provider mode, Token / Cost, and the iPhone day detail

- **Mac, provider mode** of the slide-out panel: one provider's last 30 days,
  30 daily bars with a **Token / Cost** toggle, a selected day listing its
  models (most expensive first; the day's figure is the sum of its rows),
  "Est. total · last 30 days" with its coverage and estimate lines, and the
  scope: This Mac (this Mac's logs) or All devices (the account's synced
  rows), with a switch when both have usage. Truth marks: "≥" where tokens had
  no rate, "No rate" on an unpriced model, "—" for a synced model whose cost
  is not known (never "$0.00"). Opened from a card or from a By Provider row
  of the Usage Dashboard.
- **Mac, Usage Dashboard**: the Trends chart gets the same Token / Cost
  toggle, sharing the choice.
- **Mac, the panel updates while open**: it now re-reads the usage archive on every change and
  follows the refresh's scan, so it updates while open. It used to keep the
  snapshot it opened with.
- **iPhone and iPad**: the provider card's 30-day chart gets Token / Cost, and
  a tapped day lists its models with I/O tokens and estimated cost, with the
  estimate disclaimer and the All devices note. Tap again, or ✕, to close.
- When macOS blocks this Mac's log folders, the This Mac view of Codex or
  Claude says access is needed instead of "No usage in the last 30 days".

## #668: Mac cards show Today · This Week · 30 Days with a small chart, and sections you can hide

- **Three figures** on every Mac provider card, each with tokens and cost.
  Claude still leads with messages, with I/O tokens under them. A provider
  nothing counts usage for has no usage row instead of dashes; where nothing
  counts a window the card says "—", not "$0.00".
- **A small chart** beside the figures (v1.30's 90-point chart under the card
  is gone) that follows the panel's last Token / Cost choice. Clicking it
  opens the panel in provider mode for that provider, on the card's scope and
  counting I/O as the card does.
- **Where the figures come from is on the card.** Signed out: this Mac's
  session logs ("This Mac"), so a Mac with no account now has a chart.
  Signed in: each day is the larger of this Mac's day and the account's synced
  day ("All devices"), and This Week and 30 Days are those days added up; the
  account's window figure is a floor under each cost.
- **One Today.** The Providers tab's cost bar, the Overview's Cost Summary and
  its top card now add up the same cards, so on an account used on two Macs
  they no longer show less than the card. Subscription ROI counts the
  account's 30 days.
- **Hideable sections**, per provider: Service Status, Plan and Source, Usage
  Totals, Daily Chart, Quota, Limit reset credits (Codex), Recent Sessions and
  Pace, from the card's right-click menu ("Show on Card") or Settings ›
  Providers; "Show All Sections" restores a card. Choices survive a restart
  and a language change. Hiding stops drawing only; nothing stops being read,
  refreshed or alerted on.
- The Codex card measures 233.0 pt in the store render, +1.0 pt against the
  1.54 store screenshot (budget +24).

## #671: iPhone day detail at accessibility text sizes

At AX1–AX5 each model's figures move under its name, so names wrap instead of

an iPhone 17 Pro simulator in English, Japanese and Spanish.

## #672: credentialed requests keep nothing on disk

- Every request that carries a credential (all provider collectors,
  `APIClient`'s Supabase calls including sign-in, the LoginItem helper's RPCs,
  anonymous telemetry, the reset-credits read) goes through one ephemeral
  session with no URL cache, no cookie jar and no credential store. Up to
  1.56 such requests, with their headers, bodies and answers, could be
  written to the app's and the helper's `Cache.db`.
- The Mac app, the LoginItem helper, the iPhone app and the Watch app each
  empty `URLCache.shared` and the shared cookie jar once, at the first start
  of 1.57. The done flag is written only after CFNetwork has had time to
  delete, so a process killed in between purges again next launch.
- Public requests (exchange rates, status pages, update and Companion
  manifests and downloads) are unchanged; a guard fails if one of them ever
  carries a credential.
- PRIVACY.md, Security practices, says so and names both Mac builds' cache
  folders.

## #673: scan rows read "Running", and Integrations stops offering "Session Too Long"

- **Process-scan rows (`local-` and `proc-`) are recognised in one place**
  (`SessionRecord.isProcessScanRow`). A running AI tool reads "Running", is
  counted in "N running", is titled "provider · project", and shows no usage,
  cost or requests (those were its runtime through a formula) on the Mac,
  iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch, including the iPhone/iPad session detail and
  the Watch's Live cards and detail.
- **A live process no longer drops out between refreshes**: a scan row now
  reads Running for 300 s after its last rewrite (two producer cycles plus a
  minute), up from 180 s. Every producer rewrites its rows every two minutes
  and the app refreshes every two minutes by default, so a live row could be
  240 s old on screen and was hidden. An ended process can now read Running
  for up to five minutes.
- Placeholder projects ("unknown", "local-workspace") are never shown: the
  title falls back to "<Provider> process" and the Project field to
  "Unknown", translated.
- **Integrations** (Mac) no longer offers "Long session" as a webhook event,
  since nothing raises "Session Too Long" (#656). Saved filters are left as
  stored; `send-webhook`'s alias is kept (comment only, not deployed), and
  `ci_check_alert_types.py` checks the offered and retired slugs against it.
- Demo's sessions carry their producers' ids, so Demo shows three Running rows
  with no figures, as a real account does.
- Comments and `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` corrected (no behaviour change).

## #674: iPhone and iPad Try Demo draw the card chart

Try Demo on iPhone and iPad now gives the provider cards the same synced daily
rows the Mac's Demo reads, so App Review sees the card's chart, Token / Cost
and the day detail. The rows add up to each provider's Demo figures. No other
Demo surface changes.

## #675: iPhone and iPad card Today and This Week count the chart's rows

Where the account has daily rows for a provider, the iPhone and iPad card's
Today and This Week tokens are the chart's rows added up in I/O (input +
output, labelled "I/O", Spanish "E/S"; Codex's cached input once, Claude's
cache left out), using the Mac card's rule from #668; the cost is the rows'
cost with `provider_summary`'s cost as a floor. One build feeds both the
figures and the chart. Without rows the card keeps the summary's figures,
unlabelled. Before, the figures were `provider_summary`'s input + cached +
output, which counted Codex's cached input twice and so read well above the
chart's own day. At the largest text sizes the row now moves the quota badge,
then each figure, onto lines of their own instead of breaking a figure.

- A process that names Kimi K2 (Moonshot's model) counts as Kimi, and

## Known limits

- **The iPhone Overview, the Apple Watch and the widgets still read the
  account summary** (`provider_summary` / `dashboard_summary`), which counts
  input + cached + output: the iPhone Overview's Today tile and Provider Usage
  rows, the Watch's Pulse, rings and provider detail, and the widgets. For
  Codex and Claude they read higher than the iPhone card's I/O figure (#675
  closes the gap on the card only). The server-side fix is a
  `provider_summary` / `dashboard_summary` migration planned for 1.58. With it
  also go two rare, transient differences on iPhone: Today's cost badge reads
  the summary's status while the cost beside it can be the rows' figure, and
  the cost bar above the cards stays on the summary's cost while the rows are
  newer than the summary.
- **Limit reset credits are Mac only** (the iPhone is planned later), and the
  App Store build shows them only with the `~/.codex` grant (Settings ›
  Advanced › CLI Tool Access, or "Allow access to ~/.codex" on the card).
- **Each Mac build empties only its own cache** (#672): see the Developer ID
  section; PRIVACY.md names both builds' folders.
- The iPhone and iPad Sessions legend still explains "Recent activity" and
  "Recent", which no synced session reaches any more (the Mac's own log rows
  still do).
- PDF/CSV export's "Top sessions" still ranks scan rows by their flat-rate
  estimated cost and prints their runtime-derived figures; the export is
  reworked in 1.58.
- Retired providers' Keychain items and server rows are not deleted. The
  Android app, which is not part of this release, still has a Kimi K2
  collector until its next release.
- Saved webhook filters that include `session_long` keep it until the filter
  is next saved from a Mac.
