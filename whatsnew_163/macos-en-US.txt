Signing out on this Mac now signs out only this Mac.

• Signing out on this Mac no longer signs out your iPhone and your other devices. Until now, signing out on one device signed you out on all of them. A device still running an older version of CLI Pulse signs out all your devices when you sign out on it, until it is updated.
• When this Mac had been asleep or CLI Pulse had not been used for a while, Sign Out used to leave that sign-in valid on the server. It now ends it there too.
• Crash reports no longer include an identifier derived from this Mac's hardware.
