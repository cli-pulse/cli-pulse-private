Signing out on this Mac now signs out only this Mac.

• Signing out on this Mac no longer signs out your iPhone and your other devices. Until now, it signed you out on all of them.
• Signing out on a device that still runs an older version of CLI Pulse can still sign out all your devices, until that device is updated.
• When a sign-in has expired while this Mac was asleep, Sign Out now renews it and then asks the server to end it. Before, such a sign-in could stay valid on the server. This needs a network connection.
• Crash reports no longer include an identifier derived from this Mac's hardware.
