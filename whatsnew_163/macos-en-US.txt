Signing out on this Mac now signs out only this Mac.

• Signing out on this Mac no longer signs out your iPhone and your other devices. Until now, signing out on one device signed you out on all of them.
• On a device still running an older version of CLI Pulse, signing out signs out all your devices until that device is updated.
• When this Mac had been asleep for a while, Sign Out could leave this Mac's sign-in valid on the server. It now ends it there too.
• Crash reports no longer include an identifier derived from this Mac's hardware.
